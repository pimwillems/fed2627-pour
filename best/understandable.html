BEST practice — Understandable

Clear language, descriptive links, predictable controls and helpful errors.

Demo 1

Language declared

This page starts with <html lang="en">, so screen readers pick the correct pronunciation. Foreign phrases get their own attribute: “Doe maar gewoon, dan doe je al gek genoeg.”

Demo 2

Links that describe their destination

Each link makes sense on its own — also in a screen reader’s links list:

  • Read our updated returns policy
  • Download the 2025 annual report (PDF, 2 MB)
  • Download the price list
  • Browse our current job openings
Demo 3

No surprises: user confirms the change

Browse the options freely with the arrow keys — nothing happens until you press the button.


Demo 4

Specific, helpful error messages

Submit the empty form: a summary lists every problem and links to the field, each field explains what to fix, and your input is never thrown away.

There are problems with your sign-up:

    Please enter your name.

    We only use this to confirm your registration.

    Please enter an e-mail address, like name@example.com.

    Demo 5

    Plain language

    “We refund you two days after we receive your return.”

    Short sentences, everyday words, one idea per sentence. Aim for lower-secondary reading level for general content — it helps people with cognitive disabilities, non-native speakers, people in a hurry: everyone.
