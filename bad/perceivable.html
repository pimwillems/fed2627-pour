BAD practice — Perceivable

Information must be presentable in ways users can perceive. This page gets it wrong.

This paragraph explains how to get a refund within 30 days. If you can barely read it, imagine reading it in sunlight or with low vision.

Demo 4

Meaning conveyed by colour alone

  • Payments API
  • Email service
  • Website

Which service is down? Colour-blind users (about 1 in 12 men) can't tell. Neither can a screen reader.

Start typing: the placeholder disappears and you have to remember what each field was. Screen readers may not announce placeholders at all.
